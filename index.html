Questions

Is Ask Danny free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no card to enter. You can build lists as a guest without an account. To keep it free, each connection gets a limited number of lists per day.

Which stores does it cover?

Home Depot and Lowe's for home repairs, and AutoZone for car parts.

Are the aisle numbers exact?

No. They are estimates. Store layouts differ and change after remodels, and prices will not match your receipt exactly. Items that were not matched to our product database are marked with an asterisk.

Does it tell me how to do the repair?

It tells you what to buy and what to check first, not how to do the job. For gas, electrical, structural or permitted work, hire a licensed professional.

What happens to what I type?

Your description or photo is sent to an AI provider to build the list. As a guest, your history stays in your browser. The Privacy & AI Disclosure has the details.

Who makes Ask Danny?

Matthew Sanchez, an economics student at the University of Southern California. It is a beta, so please report a wrong aisle if you find one.

Before you start

Ask Danny builds your material lists with artificial intelligence. That means it is fast and thorough, and it also means it can be wrong.

  • Aisle numbers are estimates. Layouts differ by store and change after remodels.
  • Prices are approximate and won't match your receipt exactly.
  • Items can be missed or invented. Anything marked (*) wasn't matched to our verified product database.
  • This tells you what to buy, not how to do the job. For gas, electrical, structural, or permitted work, consult a licensed professional.

What gets collected

  • What you type, photograph or record is sent to an AI company (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI or Groq) to build your list. Don't include personal or sensitive details.
  • Location is optional and only used to match regional stock. Decline it and everything still works.
  • As a guest, nothing is stored on our servers — your history stays in this browser. Make an account and it's saved to Google Firebase instead.
  • Anonymous usage stats are only collected if you accept analytics in the cookie banner. Decline and nothing is sent to Google. Either way we don't sell data and don't run ads.
